Privacy Policy for QuickStart Modern App

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Developer: LEE SANG SUN. QuickStart Modern App provides daily journaling, habit tracking, goals, and personal reflection.

Daily records and device storage

Mood entries, satisfaction ratings, notes, activity tags, goals, habits, milestones, and future letters are processed and stored in the app database on your device. This version does not send these entries to an AI service or a developer-operated server. Reflection scores, coaching messages, and scenarios use preset rules. They do not predict actual outcomes.

Advertising and third-party processing

The app uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertisements. The advertising SDK may collect and share device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostic information, and IP-derived approximate location with Google for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and service operation. These advertising data are separate from your journal entries. Please see Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising privacy information.

Retention, deletion, and choices

Local records remain on your device until removed. You can clear the app storage in Android settings to delete its local records. Device backup or transfer behavior depends on your Android settings; manage any backups separately. You can manage advertising privacy and advertising ID preferences in Android settings. Third-party advertising retention and deletion are governed by Google policies. We do not sell your journal entries and cannot access or delete records stored only on your device.

Security

Local data are stored within Android app storage. Advertising network communications use the protections provided by the Google SDK. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not directed to children.

Contact and updates

For privacy questions, contact leemanrank@gmail.com. If practices change, this policy will be updated.
