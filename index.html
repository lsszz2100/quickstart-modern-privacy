Privacy Policy for QuickStart Modern App

Effective date: September 13, 2026

QuickStart Modern App helps users record daily reflections, receive AI-powered feedback, and plan personal goals. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information we process

To provide the app’s features, we process the reflections, goals, and other content that you choose to enter. The app may also process app interaction data, device identifiers, and diagnostic information. Advertising services may process advertising identifiers and related device information to provide and measure ads.

How information is used

We use this information to provide daily logging, AI feedback, planning features, improve reliability, prevent abuse, and support advertising where shown. AI-related requests are transmitted securely to provide the requested response.

Sharing

We use service providers, including Google services for AI capabilities, app infrastructure, diagnostics, and advertising. We do not sell personal information.

Security and retention

Information is transmitted using industry-standard security measures. We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Your choices

You can avoid entering optional content. You may request access to or deletion of information associated with your use of the app by contacting us.

Children

QuickStart Modern App is intended for adults and is not directed to children.
